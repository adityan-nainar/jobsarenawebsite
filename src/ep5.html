Will AI coding agents cost more than developers?

Gartner says AI coding costs could pass a developer’s salary by 2028. We read the numbers behind the headline.

Watch the ReelDownload the cost model (.xlsx)
Misleading

True for heavy users. Not for most.

Per task, agents are far cheaper than people. The bill only passes a person’s pay when an agent runs many times the work, and the salary Gartner compares against is a global average of about $2,000 a month, well below a US developer’s.

1 · The claim

“AI coding agents will cost more than real developers”

In June 2026 Gartner forecast that AI coding costs could exceed the average developer’s salary by 2028. The salary in that comparison is a global average of about $2,000 a month.

Gartner, 24 Jun 2026, via Computer Weekly and InfoWorld · grade C

2 · Per task

Per task, agents are 90–96% cheaper

A Carnegie Mellon and Stanford study compared 48 people with 4 agent frameworks on 16 tasks. The agents cost 90.4–96.2% less per task. Their work was also lower quality.

16 tasks across data analysis, engineering, computation, writing and design, not only coding. A small sample.

Wang et al., arXiv 2510.22780, Nov 2025 · grade B

4 · What companies pay

Most pay $200–$500 a month per developer

In Gartner’s survey of tech leaders, 23% spend $200–$500 a month per developer on tokens for coding agents. About 6% spend more than $2,000.

Read through news coverage. One outlet reports 5% rather than 6% for the over-$2,000 group.

Gartner Peer Insights, Jun 2026 · grade C

What this doesn’t show

Quality: the study found agent work was lower quality, and the checking slider above is only a rough stand-in for that. Pay: the BLS figure is a US national median, and pay varies by country, company and level. Prices: token prices change fast. Gartner’s analyst described a move from $20 to $200 to $2,000 a month as tools shift from seat pricing to token pricing.
