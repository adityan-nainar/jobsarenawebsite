How we check a claim

Each episode takes one claim about AI and work that people are sharing, and checks it against the source it came from.

Five steps, every episode

  1. Pick a claim people are sharing. A headline, a stat or a forecast that is going around.
  2. Read the primary source. The report, study or dataset itself, and what it actually says.
  3. Do the maths. Every number traced to a source. Where we model something, the model is ours, kept simple and labelled.
  4. Give a verdict. One word, such as true, misleading or false, and who it holds for.
  5. Give you one thing to do, and a free file to run your own numbers.

Every source gets a grade

  • A

    Official statistics. Government data such as the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

  • B

    Published study or disclosed data. A paper or dataset you can read in full.

  • C

    News report, announcement, survey or forecast. Useful, but someone’s estimate or summary.

  • D

    Our own assumption. Labelled as ours on screen and in the file.

Rules we keep

  • No number on screen without a source.
  • Our assumptions are labelled as ours.
  • Models are simplified, never forecasts.
  • Each episode says what it doesn’t show.
  • Mistakes get fixed: DM @mr.ai.decoder and we correct the page and note the change.
